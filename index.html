Meeting recap builder

Take your notes, build a draft, and review the three sections for your Wrike recap. You can edit, move, add, or remove any item before copying.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your own meeting notes below, one point per line.
  2. Select Build recap, then review, edit, or move items into the right sections.
  3. Copy individual sections or the full recap into Wrike.

Optional labels: For me: for your tasks and Action: for follow-ups. Placements use local rules, so review them before copying. Up to 500 notes.

While editing the notes below, place the cursor in a note or select several lines, then use a button or shortcut to mark them. The same shortcuts move an item while editing its recap text.

Copy a section into its matching Wrike section, or copy the full recap with headings. Notes copy as plain lines without bullets. No tasks are posted to Wrike.

Combined recap preview

Notes and draft edits stay in this tab only. Copy your recap before closing or reloading. Rebuilding replaces the current reviewed draft.
